## 1. Role and relationship

1.1 Advisor is engaged as an independent **Strategic Advisor** to Reelin AI Inc (and its Operadroom industrial product line), not as an employee, officer, or co-founder.

1.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or agency unless expressly agreed in writing. Advisor has no authority to bind Company to contracts, pricing, or customer commitments without prior written approval from Company.

1.3 Advisory relationship is confidential until both parties agree in writing to disclose it externally (except as required by law or Advisor’s employer conflict-of-interest rules).

---

## 2. Term and time commitment

2.1 **Term:** Eighteen (18) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 **Time:** Approximately **eight (8) hours per week**, primarily on **Saturdays and Sundays**, unless otherwise agreed. Weekday hours may be used occasionally by mutual agreement.

2.3 Parties will hold a recurring product / business check-in (target: weekly or as calendared, including Fridays when useful) and a separate session to set objectives for the balance of the calendar year.

2.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement with **thirty (30) days’ written notice**. Accrued but unpaid fees for completed quarters (or pro-rata within a quarter if terminated mid-quarter by Company without cause) remain due. Vested equity remains Advisor’s; unvested equity is forfeited on termination unless Company agrees otherwise in writing.

---

## 3. Scope of advisory services

Advisor will provide strategic and technical advisory services related to Operadroom and Reelin AI’s industrial / agentic execution roadmap, including:

1. Product roadmap, ESSA / process framing, and messaging for industrial use cases  
2. Review of demos, pilots, and documentation (including Plant Memory / Vault positioning)  
3. Guidance on Management of Change (MoC), Safe Isolation, and related maintenance-execution workflows as industry pain points  
4. Positioning Operadroom as an **enabler** of existing systems of record (e.g. SAP), not a replacement  
5. Non–oil-and-gas and COI-safe verticals as agreed: including **hydrogen**, **circular economy / lithium battery recycling**, chemicals, and other sectors Advisor confirms are permissible  
6. Support preparing for a potential **Germany** presence (see Section 8)  
7. Introductions and customer-development support where COI-safe  

**Explicitly out of scope (unless later amended in writing):**

- Acting as Company’s sales lead into Shell procurement or Shell commercial bid processes  
- Using Shell confidential information belonging to Shell  
- Direct oil-and-gas customer pitches that Advisor reasonably believes violate employer COI rules  

Dominik Wagner / Rheinland commercial conversations remain Company-led. Advisor may join alignment calls as agreed.

---

## 4. Compensation — cash

4.1 Company shall pay Advisor a quarterly advisory fee of **USD $4,000 per quarter** (**$16,000 per year**), payable within **fifteen (15) days** after the end of each calendar quarter (or on a schedule otherwise agreed in writing).

4.2 Over the full 18-month term, total cash fees equal **USD $24,000** (six quarterly payments of $4,000), subject to earlier termination.

4.3 Advisor is responsible for all taxes applicable to cash fees in Advisor’s jurisdiction. Company will not withhold employment taxes unless required by law.

4.4 Reasonable pre-approved expenses (e.g. travel for Company-requested meetings) may be reimbursed with receipts.

---

## 5. Compensation — equity (Class C stock options)

5.1 Subject to Board approval and execution of Company’s standard option documents, Company will grant Advisor an option to purchase **five hundred (500) shares of Class C common stock** of Reelin AI Inc (or such Class C equity as Company designates for advisory grants).

5.2 **Vesting:** The option vests over **eighteen (18) months** from the Effective Date (or vesting commencement date stated in the option grant), in equal monthly installments (approximately **27.78 shares per month**), so long as Advisor continues to provide services under this Agreement.

5.3 **Exercise / plan terms:** Exercise price, exercise window, early exercise (if any), and other terms will follow Company’s equity incentive plan and the formal Stock Option Agreement. This Section 5 is a commercial commitment to grant; the plan documents control if there is a conflict on mechanics.

5.4 **No Shell-tied milestones:** Vesting is time-based for advisory service. Equity is **not** conditioned on closing any Shell pilot, procurement win, or named customer deal.

5.5 If Company reorganizes share classes, Advisor’s economic intent (500 Class C advisor units as of this Agreement) will be equitably preserved under the plan.

---

## 6. Conflict of interest and confidentiality

6.1 Advisor is employed by Shell (or affiliates) and must comply with employer policies. Advisor will not:

- Share Shell confidential information with Company  
- Lobby Shell procurement on Company’s behalf  
- Represent Company in Shell commercial negotiations without written COI clearance  

6.2 Advisor will promptly notify Company if a proposed task conflicts with employer rules. Parties will re-scope in good faith.

6.3 Each party will keep the other’s confidential information confidential for **three (3) years** after termination (trade secrets for so long as they remain trade secrets), except information that is public, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

6.4 Work product created by Advisor specifically for Company under this Agreement is assigned to Company upon creation, excluding Advisor’s pre-existing IP and general industry know-how.

---

## 7. Non-solicit / non-compete (limited)

7.1 During the Term and for **six (6) months** after, Advisor will not solicit Company’s employees or contractors to leave Company.

7.2 Advisor will not, during the Term, use Company confidential information to build a competing product that copies Operadroom’s specific implementations disclosed by Company. General industry advisory work for other parties is not prohibited if COI-safe and does not misuse Company confidential information.

---

## 8. Germany operations (future amendment path)

8.1 Parties intend that if Company establishes a **Germany division, branch, or subsidiary** for Operadroom / Reelin industrial operations, Advisor will be considered to **lead agreed workstreams**, which may include:

- Hydrogen / new energy  
- Circular economy and lithium battery recycling partnerships  
- Related B2B consulting and partnership motions in Germany (and nearby markets as agreed)  

8.2 Exact title, time allocation, cash, equity, and employment vs contractor status for Germany leadership will be set in a **written amendment** or separate Germany agreement when operations begin. This Section 8 is a statement of intent and good-faith priority, not a guarantee of immigration sponsorship, visa issuance, or full-time employment.

8.3 Advisor may pursue other primary employment in Germany. Company and Advisor will coordinate so advisory / Germany leadership commitments remain realistic and COI-compliant.

## 9. Business model alignment (non-binding guidance)

Parties acknowledge a dual-stream commercial approach for Operadroom:

1. **Product** (software / agentic tools, including Plant Memory and execution enablement)  
2. **Services / consulting** (implementation, process redesign, partnership add-ins to incumbent platforms)

Advisor will help Company articulate industry outcomes (e.g. MoC cycle time, Safe Isolation planning) for customers and investors. This Section is strategic guidance, not a separate SOW.
